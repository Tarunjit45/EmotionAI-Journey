> *"Exploring how artificial intelligence can understand, feel, and respond to human emotion."*

**EmotionAI Journey** is an exploratory research initiative and conceptual roadmap investigating the intersection of **Affective Computing**, **Multimodal Emotion Recognition**, and **Empathetic Human-AI Interaction**.

---

## 🧭 Motivation & Research Vision

Traditional conversational AI models are largely **transactional**—they prioritize logical task completion, syntactic correctness, and factual retrieval. However, human communication is fundamentally **relational and emotional**: over 70% of human nuance is conveyed through tone, cadence, micro-expressions, and emotional context.

The goal of EmotionAI Journey is to investigate how artificial agents can perceive emotional states accurately, adapt their cognitive responses with genuine empathy, and adhere to strict ethical guardrails that protect human well-being.

### 1. Multimodal Emotion Perception
* **Text & Conversational Sentiment:** Detecting subtle emotional valence, sarcasm, vulnerability, and implicit distress beyond surface-level keyword polarity.
* **Acoustic Prosody:** Analyzing speech rate, pitch variance, acoustic jitter, and conversational pauses to discern underlying affect.
* **Computer Vision & Micro-Expressions:** Evaluating Facial Action Coding System (FACS) units for authentic emotional signaling.

### 2. Theoretical Psychological Frameworks
* Grounding AI emotional representations in validated psychological paradigms:
  * **Russell's Circumplex Model:** Mapping feelings across orthogonal dimensions of **Valence** (pleasant to unpleasant) and **Arousal** (activation energy).
  * **Plutchik's Wheel:** Modeling primary and complex composite human emotions.

### 3. Empathetic Agent Generation
* Training conversational models to practice **perspective-taking and non-violent communication (NVC)**.
* Avoiding sycophancy or disingenuous imitation of human consciousness while maintaining compassionate, non-judgmental presence.

### 4. Ethical Safeguards & Safety Boundaries
* **Preventing Emotional Dependency:** Designing clear conversational boundaries so vulnerable users do not replace human connection with artificial companions.
* **Crisis Detection & Escalation:** Ensuring reliable escalation protocols to certified human mental health crisis lines when high-risk distress is identified.
* **Privacy of Affective Data:** Strict guarantees that biometric and emotional telemetry is never commercialized or weaponized.

## 🗺️ Project Roadmap

- [x] **Phase 1: Conceptualization & Literature Review:** Synthesizing affective computing foundational papers (Picard, Russell, Ekman).
- [ ] **Phase 2: Dataset & Benchmark Curation:** Evaluating multimodal emotion recognition datasets (IEMOCAP, AffectNet, GoEmotions).
- [ ] **Phase 3: Prototype Lightweight Emotion Classifier:** Building local-first, privacy-preserving emotion scoring on Small Language Models (SLMs).
- [ ] **Phase 4: Empathetic Dialogue Agent Proof-of-Concept:** Demonstrating adaptive conversational empathy in terminal and web interfaces.
